[3.a The Crusader army is split up in the dark.]
Nīcēa reddita est imperātōrī, et imperātor erat valdē laetus. Imperātor dēdit maximās elemosīnās nostrīs pauperibus.

Recessimus ā cīvitāte, et vēnimus ad quemdam pontem. Ibique mānsimus per duōs diēs. Tertiā diē, nostrī mīlitēs dīvīsī sunt per duo agmina. In ūnō agmine fuit vir Boamundus, et Rotbertus Normannus, et prūdēns Tancredus, et aliī plūrēs. In aliō fuit comes Sānctī Egidīī, et dux Godefridus, et Podiēnsis epīscopus, et Hugō Magnus, comesque Flandrēnsis, et aliī plūrēs.

[3.b Bohemond's army is attacked. The knights counter-attack while the foot-soldiers fortify the camp.]
Tertia diē, Turcī irruērunt vehementer super Boamundum et exercitum suum. Turcī coepērunt strīdere et garrīre ac clāmāre, cum excelsā vōce dīcentēs diabolicum sonum. Sapiēns vir Boamundus vīdit innumerābilēs Turcōs procul, et prōtinus iussit omnēs mīlitēs dēscendere, et tentōria celeriter extendere.

[3.c The Turks attack using arrows and darts; Boamund sends for aid]
Turcī undique iam erant circumcingentēs nōs, sagittandō et spīculandō. Nōn potuimus suffēre pondus tantōrum hostium. Igitur Boadmundus mīsit nūntiōs ad aliōs prīncipēs.

[3.d The Crusader Princes come to Bohemond's aid, surrounding much of the Turkish force.]
Aliī prīncipēs nōn crēdērunt nūntiōs Boadmundī. Sed Dux Godefrīdus, audāx et fortis, et Hugō Magnus, episcopus Podiēnsis, et Cōmēs dē Sānctō Egidīō perrēxērunt ad auxilium Boamundī.

Continuō fuērunt ōrdinātae nostrōrum aciēs. In sinistrā parte fuit vir sapiēns Boamundus, et Rotbertus Nortmannus, et prūdēns Tancredus, ac honestissimus mīles Robertus dē Ānsā, et Richardus dē Prīncipātū. Epīscopus vērō Podiēnsis venit per alteram montānam, undique circumcingēns incrēdulōs Turcōs. In sinistrā quoque parte equitāvit fortissimus mīles Raimundus, comes dē Sānctō Egidīō. In dexterā vērō parte fuit honōrābilis dux Godefridus, et ācerrimus mīles Flandrēnsis comes, et Hugō Magnus, et aliī plūrēs.

[3.e The Crusaders plunder the fleeing Turks. However, some of the Crusader chiefs died.]
Turcī dedērunt fugam. Fūgērunt ad sua tentōria, sed nōn licuit eōs diū morārī. Mīlitēs nostrī persecūtī sunt tōta ūnā diē. Accēpimus spolia multa: aurum, argentum, equōs et asinōs, camēlōs, ovēs, et bovēs et plūrima alia.

Sed fuērunt illīc mortuī duo ex nostrīs mīlitēs honōrābilēs, scīlicet Godefridus dē Monte Scabiōsō, et Willelmus Marchīsī fīlius, frāter Tancredī, aliīque mīlitēs et peditēs, quōrum nōmina ignōrō.

[3.f The strength and military competence of the Turks is noted.]
Quis potest dēscrībere prūdentiam, mīlitiam, et fortitūdinem Turcōrum? Turcī terrēbant multōs mīlitēs cum sagittīs eōrum. Vērumtamen Francī hoc dīcunt: nūllus homō nātūrāliter dēbet esse mīles nisi Francī et Turcī.
