[7.a The Crusaders decide to build a fortification near a mosque outside the Bridge Gate.]
Inimīcī nostrī  constrinxērunt nōs.
Nōn potuimus exīre sēcūrē. Igitur, voluimus facere castrum ad Machumāriam, quae est ante portam urbis ubi pōns est.

[7.b Bohemond and Raymond of Saint-Gilles go to the Port of Saint Simeon to get construction workers.]
Comes dē Sānctō Egidīō prīmus dīxit: "Adiuvā mē facere hoc castrum, et ego mūniam ac servābō." Boamundus respondit: "Sunt hominēs in Portō Sānctī Simeōnis quī possunt aedificāre. Dēbēmus īre ad Portum Sānctī Simeōnis et redīre cum hominibus.
Comes igitur et Boamundus perrēxērunt ad Portum Sānctī Simeōnis .

[7.c The Turks notice that Boahmund and Raymond are away and then attack.]
Turcī vīdērunt quod maiōrēs nostrī dēessent.
Irruērunt super peditēs nostrōs prope cīvitātem, sagittandō et vulnerandō.
Peditēs nostrī dedērunt fugam. Turcī occīdērunt plūs quam mīlle.
Aliī peditēs mortuī sunt, aliī fūgērunt in montānam.

[7.d Bohemond returns early, putting the Turks to flight, many of whom die trying to cross a narrow bridge.]
Boamundus reverit celeriter et irruit vehementer super Turcōs.
Turcī fūgērunt per medium angustī pontis, sed nōn potuērunt fugere celeriter quia pōns erat angustus.
Unda flūminis fluit rubea cum sanguine Turcōrum.

[7.e The Turks bury their dead by the Machumāria, but the Crusaders dig up and plunder the bodies.]
Crāstinā diē, aliī Turcī exiērunt dē cīvitāte et colligērunt omnia cadāvera Turcōrum mortuōrum.
Sepeliērunt cadāvera ad Machumāriam, quae est ultrā pontem ante portam urbis.
Simulque cōnsepeliērunt pallia, bisantēōs aureōs, arcūs, sagittās, et alia plūrima īnstrūmenta.
Nostrī mīlitēs vēnērunt et aperuērunt tumbās eōrum et trāxērunt eōs extrā sepulchra.
Et ēiēcērunt omnia cadāvera eōrum in quandam foveam.
Tertiā diē coepimus, cum gaudiō magnō, aedificāre castrum ad Machumāriam, dē lapidibus quōs abstrāximus dē tumulīs Turcōrum.

[7.f The finished castle near the Machumāria lets the Crusaders forage safely.]
Tunc habēbāmus castrum prope pontem, et Turcī nōn potuērunt exīre contrā nōs.
Nōs autem sēcūrē ambulābāmus hūc et illūc,
ad portam et ad montanēās, laudantēs et glōrificantēs Dominum Deum nostrum.
