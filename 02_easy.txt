[2.a Bohemond prevents seizure of a castle.]
      Imperātor mīsit nūntium, ut nūntius dēdūceret sēcūrē per terram suam ad Cōnstantīnopolim.
      Venīmus ad quaddam castrum, sed habitātōrēs castrī nōs timēbant et nōn volēbant vēndere nōs mercātum. Ergō, nostrī mīlitēs voluērunt apprehendere castrum. Sed prūdēns vir Boamundus nōn permīsit nōs apprehendere castrum, prō iūstitiā terrae et fīdūcia imperātōris. In māne, habitātōrēs castrī vēnērunt in praesentiam Boamundī et vēndēbant mercātum.
      
[2.b Bohemond returns some cattle.]
      Deinde vēnimus ad quamdam urbem quae dīcitur Serra. In Serra fīximus tentōria, et habuimus satis mercātum. Doctus Boamundus, prō amīcitiā et prō iūstitiā terrae, iussit reddī omnia animālia quae nostrī depredāta tenēbant.
      Deinde pervēnimus ad Rūsam cīvitātem. Gēns Graecōrum gaudēns veniēbat in occursum dominī Boamundī, et vēndēbat nōs mercātum.

[2.c Bohemond ventures ahead and leaves Tancred in charge of the army.]
      Doctus Boamundus dīmīsit gentem suam in Rūsā, et perrēxit loquī cum imperātōre in  Cōnstantīnopolim. Boamundus dūxit sēcum paucōs mīlitēs. Tancredus remānsit in Rūsā et erat caput mīlitiae Chrīstī. Tancredus condūxit populum in vallem quamdam plēnam omnibus bonīs. In hāc valle celebrāvimus dēvōtissimē Pascha Dominī.
      
[2.d The Emperor Alexius plots against the Crusader Princes.]
      Honestissimus vir Boamundus venit ad imperātōrem, et imperātor iussit eum honōrābiliter recipī, et cautē hospitārī extrā urbem. Malignus imperātor voluit loquī sēcrētō cum Boadmundō. Tunc Godefridus et Cōmēs Sānctī Egidīī venit ad cīvitātem et hospitātī sunt prope Boamundum. Anxiēns imperātor volēbat callidē et fraudelenterque comprehenderet hōs mīlitēs Chrīstī. Sed, dīvīna grātia, nōn potuit.
      
[2.e The Emperor tries to get them to swear an oath and promises Bohemond a fief.]
      Omnēs maiōrēs Graecōrum, timentēs nē prīvārentur suā patriā, volēbant maiōrēs nostrōs iūrāre sacrāmentum. Maiōrēs nostrī nōn voluērunt iūrāre fīdūciam imperātōrī.
      Sed, compulsī necessitāte, maiōrēs nostrī humiliāvērunt sē ad voluntātem nēquissimī imperātōris. Imperātor prōmīsit Boamundō terram magnam prope Antiochīam.
      Imperātor quoque prōmīsit omnibus nostrīs fidem, sēcūritātem, et mercātum in viā Sānctī Sepulchrī.
      
[2.f Raymond of Saint-Gilles refuses to swear an oath of vassalage.]
      Comes Sānctī Egidīī nōn voluit iūrāre sacrāmentum imperātōrī, et voluit pugnāre contrā imperātōrem. Sed dux Godefridus, Rotbertus comes Flandrēnsis, et aliī princepēs dīxērunt eī: "nōn dēbēs pugnāre contrā Chrīstiānōs'. Igitur comes, acceptō cōnsiliō ā suīs, iūrāvit Alexīō vītam et honōrem, sed nōn iūrāvit dē homīniō.
      
[2.h The Crusader princes cross the Bosporos, and Bohemond organizes food shipments.]
      Posteā, Tancredus et aliī prīncipēs trānsfretāvērunt Brāchium. Boamundus remānsit cum imperātōre et loquēbantur quōmodo mandārent mercātum gentibus quae erant
ultrā Nicēnam cīvitātem.

[2.i Godfrey expands the road to Nicea.]
Via ad Nicenam erat valdē parva et angusta. Igitur, dux Godefridus mīsit ante sē tria mīlia hominum cum secūribus et gladiīs. Hiī mīlitēs aperiērunt viam, et pōnēbant crucēs ferreās ac ligneās in viā; crucēs dēmōnstrābant viam ad Nicenam.

[2.j The soldiers arrive at Nicea; Bohemond brings food by sea.]
Posteā, pervēnimus ad Nīcēam. Erat inopia pānis, et ūnus pānis vēnderētur vīgintī aut
trīgintā dēnāriīs. Postquam vir prūdēns Boamundus vēnit et iussit maximum mercātum condūcī per mare. Nāvēs trahērunt cibum, et fuit maxima ūbertās in tōtā mīlitā Chrīstī.

[2.k The Crusaders besiege Nicea; a Turkish relief army approaches.]
In diē autem Ascēnsiōnis Dominī invādere coepimus urbem.
Coepimus aediﬁcare īnstrūmenta lignōrum atque turrēs ligneās, quō possēmus turrēs sternere. Sed Turcī, quī erant in urbe, mīsērunt nūntiōs per portam. Mīlitēs nostrī nōn erant prope hanc portam; ergō nūntiī exiit per portam secūre. Deinde, nūntiī redidērunt ad cīvitātem cum exercitū magnō.

[2.l Raymond of Saint-Gilles defeats the Turkish relief army, beheading them and catapulting their heads into Nicea.]
Comes dē Sānctō Egidīō invēnit Turcōs venientēs contrā nōs.
Cum exercitū suō fortissimō irruit super illōs atque superāvit.
Comes dētruncābat multōs Turcōs. Cum fundā, prōiciēbant capita eōrum in urbem, ut inde Turcī magis terrērentur.

[2.m Raymond of Saint-Gilles undermines a wall, but the Turks repair it overnight.]
Dēnique comes sānctī Egidīī et epīscopus Podiēnsis suffōdērunt turrim. Mīlitēs eōrum fōdērunt mūrum, et arbalistae et sagittāriī defendābant eōs. Submīsērunt ligna sub mūrō ac deinde mīsērunt ignem. Turris cecidit in nocte. Sed Turcī, in nocte, restaurāvērunt mūrum fortiter et, in māne, nōn possuimus intrāre cīvitātem.

[2.n More Crusaders arrive and complete the land siege.]
Vēnit comes dē Nortmannīā, aliī plūrēs. Boamundus dēnique obsēdit urbem in prīmā fronte, et iuxtā eum Tancredus, et posteā dux Godefridus, ac deinde comes Flandrēnsis,
iuxtā quem Rotbertus Nortmannus, et iuxtā eum comes Sānctī Egidīī, iuxtā quem Podiēnsis epīscopus. Ita obsīdēbāmus tōtam cīvitātem. Turcī nōn potuērunt intrāre neque exīre per terram.

[2.o The emperor sends boats to complete the water siege of Nicea.]
Sed, erat immēnsus lacus prope urbem. Turcī potuērunt intrāre vel exīre cīvitātem in nāvēs per lacum. Nostrī maiōrēs mīsērunt nūntiōs ad Cōnstantīnopolim. Imperātor mīsit nāvēs, plēnās Turcopōlīs et bene ōrnātīs armīs. Turcī vīdērunt magnum numerum nāvium et timuērunt usque ad mortem.

[2.p The Turks surrender Nicea to the emperor.]
Turcī, quī erant in Nīcēan, vīdērunt quod nōn possent habēre adiūtōrium. Mīsērunt lēgātiōnem imperātōrī et reddidērunt cīvitātem. Imperātor permīsit eōs abīre cum mulieribus et fīliīs et omnibus substantiīs suīs.

Fuimusque in obsidiōne illa per septem ebdomadās et trēs diēs. Multī ex nostrīs illīc recēpērunt martyrium et reddidērunt felicēs animās Deō.
