[4.a Solimānus flees from Nicea and complains about the crusaders.]
Turcī fūgērunt per quattuor diēs et noctēs. Solimānus, dux illōrum, invēnit decem mīlia Arabum. Ārabēs dīxērunt eī: "Ō īnfēlīx vir! Cūr tremefactus fugis?" Solimānus lacrimābiliter respondit: "Quia voluī superāre Francōs, sed potuī superāre Francōs, tunc vīdī retrō et vīdī innumerābilem gentem eōrum. Nōn possumus superāre tam magnum exercitum Francōrum. Ergō, fugitē!" Et Solimānus et Ārabēs fūgērunt per Romāniam.

[4.b The Turks loot the Christian cities on the way out.]
Tunc veniēbāmus nōs persequentēs inīquissimōs Turcōs, cotīdiē fugientēs ante nōs.
Turcī veniēbant ad castra et urbēs, dēlūdentēs habitātōrēs, dīcēbant: "Superāvimus omnēs Chrīstiānōs,
nēmō potest vōs aduīvāre. Ergō, permittite nōs intus."
Turcī intrābant castra et urbēs et spoliābant ecclēsiās et domōs et alia omnia.
Dūcēbant equōs sēcum et asinōs et mūlōs, aurum et argentum et ea quae reperīre poterant.
Quoque, tollēbant fīliōs Chrīstiānōrum sēcum.
Et ārdēbant ac dēvastābant omnia convenientia sīve ūtilia.

[4.c A lack of food and water causes many of the Crusader's horses to die.]
Nōs itaque persequēbāmur eōs per dēsertā et inaquōsam et inhabitābilem terram.
Nōn erat multum cibum vel aquam.
Illīc fuit mortua maxima pars nostrōrum equōrum.

[4.d The Crusaders re-supply at Iconium, then defeat some Turks at Heraclea Cybistra.]
Intrāvimus terram optimam plēnam corporālibus alimentīs et dēliciīs omnibusque bonīs.
Appropinquāvimus Īconiō.
Habitātōrēs terrae illīus dīxērunt nōbīs: "Ferte vōbīscum uturēs plēnōs aqua, quia illīc in itinere diēī ūnīus nōn est multa aqua."
Pervēnērunt ad Erachiam. Erat magna congregātiō Turcoroum.
Nostrī mīlitēs pugnābant fortiter contrā Turcōs et superāvērunt illōs.

[4.e Tancred puts the Turks to flight outsīde the Cilician Gates. The Turks flee under cover of night.]
Tancred venit Tarsum cum suīs mīlitibus.
Exiērunt Turcī dē urbe, et vēnērunt obviam eīs, pugnābant contrā Chrīstiānōs.
Tancredus superāvit illōs. Turcī dedērunt fugam et revertērunt in urbem.
Tancredus, vir prūdēns atque honōrābilis mīles Chrīstī, castramētātus est
ante portam urbis.
Nox venit, et omnēs Turcī fūgērunt urbem.

[4.f Tancred and Baldwin dispute over ownership of Tarsus.]
In māne, maiōrēs cīvitātis reddidērunt sponte cīvitātem et dīxērunt: "volumus Tancredum rēgnāre nōs, quia heri virīliter pugnāvit cum Turcīs."
Balduīnus voluit spoliāre cīvitātem.
Fortissimus Tancredus dīxit: "Nōn! ego nōlō expoliāre Chrīstiānōs."
Sed, Balduīnus habēbat magnum exercitum; ergō, Tancredus recessit cum suō exercitū.
Et duae cīvitātēs, Athēna et Manustra, trāditae sunt Tancrēdō.

[4.g The Crusaders allow Simeon the Armenian to retain control over a castle.]
Raimundus, comes dē Sānctō Egidīō, et doctissimus Boamundus, duxque Godefridus, et aliī prīncipēs intrāvērunt terram Hermeniōrum.
Pervēnērunt ad quoddam castrum. Turcī nōn potuērunt superāre hoc castrum.
Erat ibi homō, nōmine Simeōn, quī dīxit prīncipibus: "Datē mihi hoc castrum, quia ego defendīvī hoc castrum nōn Turcōs,
et Turcī nōn potuērunt mē superāre."
Et prīncipēs dedērunt eī castrum. Et Simeōn remānsit cum suā gente.

[4.h The Crusaders give a city outside Cappadocia to Petrus dē Alpibus.]
Pervēnimus usque Cesaream Cappadociae.
Exīvimus ā Cappadociā et vēnimus ad quamdam cīvitātem pulcherrimam.
Turcī obsēderant hanc cīvitātem per trēs ebdomadās, sed nōn superāverant.
Erat mīles, cui nōmen Petrus dē Alpibus, quī erat in cīvitātem et pugnāverat fortiter contrā Turcōs.
Igitur cīvitās sē trāditur.

[4.i Raymond of Saint-Gilles prepares for the siege of Antioch.]
Turcī erant in cūstōdiā Antiochīae.
Nostrī mīlitēs vēnērunt itaque in vallem prope Antiocham.

Petrus dē Roasā dīvīsit sē ibi ab aliīs.
Proximā nocte trānsīvit prope Antiochīam, intrāvitque vallem dē Rugīā et invēnit Turcōs et Saracēnōs, et praelīātus
est cum eīs et occīdit multōs ex eīs.

[4.j The Crusaders travel through a narrow mountain pass with unsufficient pack animals.]
Nōs autem quī remānsimus, intrāvimus in diabolicam montānam.
Erat alta et angusta. Equī praecipitābant illīc.
Necesse est portāre armās in manibus. Multī mīlitēs vēndēbant clipeōs et lōrīcās,
quia nōn volēbant portāre eōs per angustam montānam.

[4.k The Crusaders leaved the mountain and arrive near Antioch. The rē-supply and prepāre for the siege.]
Exīvērunt dē exsecrāta montāna et pervēnimus ad cīvitātem quae vocātur Marasim.
Vēnērunt itaque nostrī mīlitēs in vallem, in quā rēgālis cīvitās Antiochīa sita est.
Praeparāmus pugnāre contrā Turcōs.
