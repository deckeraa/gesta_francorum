[8.a The Crusaders decide to take another castle outside Antioch. Tancred volunteers.]
Turci non potuerunt exire civitatem, nisi ex illā parte ﬂūminis, ubi erat castrum et quoddam monastērium. Igitur, milites nostri volebant munire et tenere illud castrum.
Tancredus dixit: "Ero ad castrum et tenēbō." Et alii milites spoponderunt ei quattuor centum marcās argentī.

[8.b Tancred captures a Turkish re-supply mission being carried out by Armenians and Syrians.]
Tancredus remansit ibi cum suis militibus, et coepit coangustare civitatem vehementer.
Ipsā quoque diē veniēbat magna pars Hermeniōrum et Surānōrum sēcūrē dē montaneīs,
quī ferēbant alimenta Turcīs in adiūtōrium cīvitātis.
Tancredus apprehendit eos et omnia quae deferebantt, vidēlicet frūmentum, vīnum, et alia huiusmodī.

[8.c An emir named Pirus offers to hand over a tower to Bohemond in exchange for money and freedom.]
Erat quīdam ammirātus in Antiochiā cui nōmen Pirus.
Boamundus saepe mittebat nuntios Pirō.
Boamundus promittebat libertatem et pecuniam Pirō sī Pirus daret eī turrem quam custodebat.
Et Pirus promittebat dare turrem Boamundō.

[8.d Bohemond suggests to the other Crusader Princes that the city should go to whomever can first capture it; however, they demand it to be sold equally.]
Boamundus, iam sēcūrus dē introitū cīvitātis, vēnit ad omnes seniores.
Dīxit: "Nos omnes in paupertate et miseria sumus. Igitur, si unus ex nobis potuerit adquirere civitatem aliquo modo ingenio, concedamus urbem ei."
Sed seniores dēnegāvērunt: "Non, sed habēbimus civitatem aequē."

[8.e Due to an large incoming Turkish relief force, the other Crusader Princes change their mind, saying that if Alexios didn't come to their aid then Bohemond could have the city.]
Nōn multō post audīvimus nūntiōs dē exercitū hostium nostrōrum.
Multi Turci veniebant ad Antiocham.
Maiores nostri igitur dixerunt: "Si imperator vēnerit nōbīs in adiūtōrium, reddemus civitatem ei; sin, autem, Boamundus habeat civitatem in suam potestātem.
 
[8.f Bohemond sends a messenger to Pirus and he agrees to hand over the tower.]
Boamundus misit nuntium Piro, et dixit: "Milites nostri ierunt in terram Saracenorum, sed celeriter revertent furtim per dextram montāneam."
Pirus dīxit: "Dabō tibi turrem."

[8.g That night, a ladder is placed and 60 Franks ascend. The ladder breaks, but the Franks are able to open a gate to let more of their soldiers in.]
Illā nocte, sexaginta milites Boamundī ascendit per scalam in turrem.
Milites clamabant "Deus vult! Deus vult!" et pugnabant contra Turcos.
Intereā forte rupta est scāla. Sed milites qui erant intus aperuerunt portam, et multes Franci intraverunt civitatem.
Boamundus posuit vexillum suum coram castellum in Antiocham.

[8.h Seeing Bohemond's banner, some Turks, including the Emir Cassiānus, begin fleeing the city. Some Armenians and Syrians catch Cassiānus and behead him.]
Turci viderunt vexillum Boamundi et audierunt clamorem et fugerunt civitatem.
Emir Cassiānus fugit civitatem. Surānī et Hermenīī et apprehendērunt eum.
Truncāvēruntque caput illīus et tulērunt ante praesentiam Boamundī.

[8.i The streets of Antioch are filled with dead bodies. Some Turks remain in the city's citadel.]
Viae Antiochae sunt plenae cadaveribus mortuorum.
Nūllus vērō poterat īre per sēmitam cīvitātis, nisī super cadāvera mortuōrum.
Sed alii Turci vivi remansit in castello in mediā Antiochiae.
