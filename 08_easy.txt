[8.a The Crusaders decide to take another castle outside Antioch. Tancred volunteers.]
Turcī nōn potuērunt exīre cīvitātem, nisi ex illā parte flūminis, ubi erat castrum et quoddam monastērium. Igitur, mīlitēs nostrī volēbant mūnīre et tenēre illud castrum. Tancredus dīxit: "Erō ad castrum et tenēbō." Et aliī mīlitēs spopondērunt eī quattuor centum marcās argentī.

[8.b Tancred captures a Turkish re-supply mission being carried out by Armenians and Syrians.]
Tancredus remānsit ibi cum suīs mīlitibus, et coepit coangustāre cīvitātem vehementer. Ipsa quoque diē veniēbat magna pars Hermeniōrum et Surānōrum sēcūrē dē montanēīs, quī ferēbant alimenta Turcis in adiūtōrium cīvitātis. Tancredus apprehendit eōs et omnia quae dēferēbant, vidēlicet frūmentum, vīnum, et alia huiusmodī.

[8.c An emir named Pirus offers to hand over a tower to Bohemond in exchange for money and freedom.]
Erat quīdam ammīrātus in Antiochīā cui nōmen Pirus. Boamundus saepe mittēbat nūntiōs Pirō. Boamundus prōmittēbat lībertātem et pecūniam Pirō sī Pirus daret eī turrem quam custodēbat. Et Pirus prōmittēbat dare turrem Boamundō.

[8.d Bohemond suggests to the other Crusader Princes that the city should go to whomever can first capture it; however, they demand it to be sold equally.]
Boamundus, iam sēcūrus dē introitū cīvitātis, venit ad omnēs seniōrēs. Dīxit: "Nōs omnēs in paupertāte et miseriā sumus. Igitur, sī ūnus ex nōbīs potuerit adquīrere cīvitātem aliquō modo ingeniō, concēdāmus urbem eī." Sed seniōrēs dēnegāvērunt: "Nōn, sed habēbimus cīvitātem aequē."

[8.e Due to an large incoming Turkish relief force, the other Crusader Princes change their mind, saying that if Alexios didn't come to their aid then Bohemond could have the city.]
Nōn multō post audīvimus nūntiōs dē exercitū hostium nostrōrum. Multī Turcī veniēbant ad Antiocham. Maiōrēs nostrī igitur dīxērunt: "Sī imperātor vēnerit nōbīs in adiūtōrium, reddēmus cīvitātem eī; sīn, autem, Boamundus habeat cīvitātem in suam potestātem.

[8.f Bohemond sends a messenger to Pirus and he agrees to hand over the tower.]
Boamundus mīsit nūntium Pirō, et dīxit: "Mīlitēs nostrī iērunt in terram Saracēnōrum, sed celeriter revertent fūrtim per dextram montāneam." Pirus dīxit: "Dabō tibi turrem."

[8.g That night, a ladder is placed and 60 Franks ascend. The ladder breaks, but the Franks are able to open a gate to let more of their soldiers in.]
Illā nocte, sexāgintā mīlitēs Boamundī ascendērunt per scālam in turrem. Mīlitēs clāmābant "Deus vult! Deus vult!" et pugnābant contrā Turcōs. Intereā forte rupta est scāla. Sed mīlitēs quī erant intus aperuērunt portam, et multī Francī intrāvērunt cīvitātem. Boamundus posuit vēxillum suum cōram castellō in Antiochiā.

[8.h Seeing Bohemond's banner, some Turks, including the Emir Cassiānus, begin fleeing the city. Some Armenians and Syrians catch Cassiānus and behead him.]
Turcī vīdērunt vēxillum Boamundī et audiērunt clāmōrem et fūgērunt ex cīvitāte. Emir Cassiānus fugit cīvitātem. Surānī et Hermēniī apprehendērunt eum. Truncāvēruntque caput illīus et tulērunt ante praesentiam Boamundī.

[8.i The streets of Antioch are filled with dead bodies. Some Turks remain in the city's citadel.]
Viae Antiochae sunt plēnae cadāveribus mortuōrum. Nūllus vērō poterat īre per sēmitam cīvitātis, nisi super cadāvera mortuōrum. Sed aliī Turcī vīvī remānsērunt in castellō in mediā Antiochīae.
