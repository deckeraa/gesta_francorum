[5.a The Crusaders defeat a Turkish army near the Orontes River.]
Venīmus ad Pontem Farream.
Nostrī cursōrēs invēnērunt Turcōs innumerābilēs congregātōs obviam eīs.
Turcī voluērunt dare adiūtōrium Antiochīae.
Nostrī mīlitēs igitur irruērunt Turcōs et superāvērunt eōs.
Turcī dedērunt fugam.
Nostrī mīlitēs accēpērunt spolia multa: equōs, camēlōs, mūlōs, asinōs, onustōs frūmentō et vīnō.

[5.b The Crusaders begin the siege of Antioch. After spending out spies, the Turci begin laying ambushes.]
Obsēdimus Antiochīam. Turcī erant perterritī, et nōn audēbant exīre cīvitātem.
Sed mīsērunt Hermēniōs et Surianīōs, ostendentēs sē fugere.
Et investīgābant nostrum exercitum.
Tunc Turcī coepērunt pugnāre contrā nōs ex urbe.

[5.c Boamund defeats a Turkish force sallying out of a Turkish castle.]
Erat autem nōn longē castrum cui nōmen Aregh, ubi erant congregātī multī
Turcī fortissimī.
Boamundus surrēxit cum suīs mīlitibus et iērunt prope castrum.
Turcī irruērunt super illōs. Sed Boamundus superāvit illōs.
Paucī ex inimīcīs captī sunt et ductī sunt ante portam urbis;
ibique dēcollābantur, ut magis trīstēs fierent quī erant in urbe.

[5.d Food becomes scarce and the Crusaders organize a foraging party.]
Frūmentum coeperat esse carum ante Nātāle Dominī.
Ergō Boamundus dīxit: "Ego, cum Flandrensī comite, ībimus in terram Saracēnōrum et redībimus cum cibō et aliīs spoliīs."
Boamundus et Cōmēs Flandrēnsis intrāvērunt terram Saracēnōrum et invēnērunt magnum exercitum Turcōrum.
Turcī sē dīvīsēruntque et fēcērunt duās aciēs, ūnam ante et aliam retrō.
Volēbant circumcingere nōs.

[5.e The Count of Flanders puts the Turks to flight. The Crusaders return the spoils to their sieging party.]
Itaque comes Flandrēnsis occurrit illīs ūna cum Boamundō.
Nostrī mīlitēs irruērunt ūnanimiter super illōs.
Turcī dedērunt fugam.
Nostrī mīlitēs cēpērunt equōs eōrum et alia spolia.
Rediērunt cum magnō gaudiō et laudāvimus Deum.
